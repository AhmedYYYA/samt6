## Design assumptions for MOD consideration

These are recommendations, not answers attributed to the proposer or approved MOD policy.

- Retain the reference 3/6/3/4-week station allocation. Plan contact time and transitions inside the 16-week calendar. The archived earlier page used a different travel-time convention; this point must be settled in detailed scheduling before launch.
- Keep a 12-month follow-up baseline with eight mentoring touchpoints. Capacity starts at 10 in the demo and can be adjusted up to 20.
- Keep Sandhurst, RAF Cosford, Tawazun, EDGE, GAL, TII and EDGE Europe as named anchors. Retain Defence Academy/Cranfield and DSCA/DSCU, war-college and Naval Postgraduate School tracks found in the earlier SAMT6 page. All hosting remains subject to agreement.
- Use eight competency domains and proposed assessment weights. They require pilot calibration.
- Demonstrate institutional nomination and panel-led selection. The earlier archived page described direct applications and specific thresholds. These are not treated as approved policy; the final application channel and standards belong in MOD's design decision.
- Use a shared learning core with role-appropriate assignments and mixed military/civilian project teams.
- Treat the first decision as in-principle endorsement and commissioning of feasibility work, followed by a separate funded pilot-launch decision.
- Use illustrative budget inputs only. No cost estimate is attributed to a host and no launch date is claimed.

## Identity

Plum, vermilion and white; typographic Arabic identity; structured editorial proposal and a practical programme workspace. The mark is an abstract upward progression, replacing the former compass interface. No MOD insignia or partner logos imply institutional endorsement.
